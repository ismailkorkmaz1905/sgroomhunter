Operator Timeline
1. Worker scans PropertyGuru feeds on schedule.

3. Telegram receives a review card with Approve / Reject actions.
4. If approved, WhatsApp draft is opened or auto-sent.
5. Sent messages stay visible long enough for cross-device sync.
