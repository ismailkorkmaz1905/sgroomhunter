About

RoomRadar helps renters move faster on the listings that actually fit.

Instead of forcing users to manually triage hundreds of room listings, RoomRadar translates preferences into filters, explains every rejection, and prepares outreach when a match appears.

What It Does

Structured Criteria

Users define budget, neighborhoods, bathroom rules, cooking needs, owner-stay preferences, and more.

Listing Qualification

Each listing is labeled as qualified, risky, or rejected with concrete reasons.

WhatsApp Outreach

Users can open ready-made WhatsApp messages for approved listings in one click.

Business Model

Subscriptions

Charge for advanced profiles, higher alert volume, and better automation.

Partner Revenue

Monetize with relocation services, broadband, insurance, and moving-related referrals.

Future Ad Inventory

High-intent renter traffic is a natural fit for AdSense and curated sponsor placements.
