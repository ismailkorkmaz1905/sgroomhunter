Privacy

Privacy-first by default.

This demo explains the kind of data RoomRadar would collect and how it would be used in a production service.

RoomRadar stores search criteria, listing decisions, and outreach drafts so users can manage their housing search more efficiently.

We would only use contact and preference data to provide listing alerts, qualification logic, and messaging workflows requested by the user.

We do not sell personal data. Aggregate analytics and future ad measurement may be used to improve performance and monetization.

If ad systems such as Google AdSense are added later, consent handling, cookie disclosures, and region-specific compliance flows should be implemented before launch.

Users should be able to export or delete their profile data from the product dashboard.
